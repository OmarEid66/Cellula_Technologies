Paper C: A Shallow CNN Baseline

Abstract
We evaluate a shallow baseline convolutional network. It reaches a CNN accuracy of 89% on CIFAR-10 with
0.4 million parameters, showing that small models remain competitive for constrained devices.

Methodology
The baseline has five convolutional layers and no residual connections. Training uses Adam for 60 epochs.
The computational cost is 0.1 GFLOPs per image.

Results
The CNN accuracy on CIFAR-10 is 89%. Precision is 0.88 and recall is 0.89.
