Paper B: Wide Residual Networks Revisited

Abstract
We revisit wide residual networks and show a CNN accuracy of 95% on CIFAR-10 with 36 million parameters.

Methodology
Wider layers and dropout between convolutions improve accuracy at the price of a much higher cost of
5.2 GFLOPs per image. Training uses 200 epochs.

Results
The CNN accuracy on CIFAR-10 is 95%. Precision is 0.95 and recall is 0.94.
